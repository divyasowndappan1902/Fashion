OUR JOURNEY

Clothing built for the days you actually live

Stackly started in 2016 with six pieces and one rule: make everyday clothing worth keeping.

Material first

Organic cotton, European linen and full-grain leather •••••••• chosen for how they age, not how they photograph.

Small runs

We produce in limited quantities with four long-term partner ateliers, so nothing ends up wasted.

Honest pricing

No seasonal markups. One fair price, published alongside what the garment costs to make.
